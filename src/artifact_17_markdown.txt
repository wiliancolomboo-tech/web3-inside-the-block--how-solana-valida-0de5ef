1/4 🧵 Ever wondered why your high-priority Solana tx STILL gets dropped during high volatility? 

Solana does NOT have an EVM-style mempool, nor does it run on pure First-Come-First-Served (FCFS). 

Here is what ACTUALLY happens inside the block during validator BankingStage 👇

2/4 ⚙️ A Solana validator's Central Scheduler orchestrates transactions across worker threads using a conflict graph:
- 48M Compute Units per block limit
- 12M CU write-lock limit per individual account!
- Priority is scored in micro-lamports per CU (`Fee / Requested CU`)

If you hit a saturated AMM pool, no amount of priority fee can override the 12M lock ceiling.

3/4 🚀 Enter Jito-Solana: Over 85% of validator stake runs Jito, which interleaves atomic searcher bundles ahead of native TPU packets via out-of-band auctions. If your transaction conflicts with bundle write locks, you are parked in a stall queue!
