Ever wondered what actually happens inside a Solana block in the 400ms before it's finalized? 

It’s NOT an EVM-style serial queue. Solana uses Sealevel parallel scheduling, dynamic priority fees, and read/write account lock graphs to execute thousands of transactions simultaneously.

Here is how it works under the hood 👇

🧵 2/3
1️⃣ Packets enter via QUIC & SigVerifyStage
2️⃣ BankingStage parses Compute Budget micro-lamports/CU
3️⃣ Transactions writing to DISJOINT accounts run concurrently across multi-threaded workers
4️⃣ Jito out-of-band auction bundles get atomic precedence
5️⃣ PoH Recorder ticks state into an immutable stream

When two txs touch the same AMM pool? Lock contention strikes.
