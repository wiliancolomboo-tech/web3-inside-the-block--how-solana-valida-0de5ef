1/7 🧵 Inside the Block: How do Solana validators REALLY order transactions? 
Forget standard EVM mempools and PBS. Solana processes transactions through Gulf Stream, QUIC TPUs, and Sealevel parallel runtimes. Here is the engineering reality under the hood 👇 ⚡

2/7 ⚙️ In stock Agave (@solanalabs), transactions enter the BankingStage multi-threaded worker channels. They are sorted into a Max-Heap based on priority fee rate (micro-lamports / CU). But here’s the catch: parallel execution is bounded by Sealevel account write-locks!

3/7 🔒 If your swap writes to a hot account (e.g. Raydium/Orca AMM), you hit the hard 12,000,000 Compute Unit per-account lock cap. No matter how much priority fee you pay, if that account's lock is saturated in the current slot, your tx is deferred or dropped.

4/7 🚀 What about @jito_labs? Over 85% of Solana stake runs Jito-Solana. Searchers submit atomic bundles directly to out-of-protocol Block Engines. These bundles skip standard BankingStage queues and are prepended right at the HEAD of the block before regular TPU transactions.
